Grind Only v1.0.1 - mailbox gold tracking fix

Fixes a possible permanent loot/money suppression after visiting a mailbox
on WoW Forever clients that fail to deliver MAIL_CLOSED consistently.

Checks whether MailFrame is actually visible on each loot or money event,
clearing a stale mailbox flag once the frame has been closed. Also resets
mailbox state when entering the world. While mailbox is visibly open,
mail attachments are still excluded from grinding income.

Includes v1.0.0 history and level-aware scoring improvements.
SavedVariables are unchanged.
